Privacy policy

Last updated: [date]

Velocity Travels ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what personal data we collect when you contact us, why we use it, and the choices you have. We handle personal data in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Contact details: your name, mobile number, email address if you give it, and how and when you prefer to be reached.
  • Trip details: the type of trip, pickup and destination, dates, number of travellers, vehicle preference and any notes you add.
  • Business details: for corporate enquiries, your company or institution name, routes, timings and invoice needs.
  • How you found us: the website or advert that brought you here and the first page you opened, sent with your enquiry. We don't use cookies for this.

Why we use it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote, arrange and run your trip, and meet our legal and accounting duties.

How your enquiry reaches us

When you send the form, your enquiry is saved in our private Google Sheet and emailed to our team. If you gave an email address, we may send you a confirmation. Google provides these services and processes the data for us, and may store it outside India. If you choose to message us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp's own privacy policy also applies.

While you fill in the form, a draft is saved only on your own device for up to 14 days, so you don't lose your answers. It is removed when you finish the form or choose "Start over".

Who we share it with

We share only what is needed with Google as described above, with the driver or partner serving your trip, and with authorities where the law requires it. We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for up to [12 months], and longer only where tax or legal rules require it.

Your rights

You can ask us for a summary of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct or erase it, withdraw your consent, nominate someone to act for you, and raise a grievance. Write to us using the details below. You may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
